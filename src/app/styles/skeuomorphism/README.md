# Skeuomorphism

Skeuomorphism is interface design that imitates physical materials and objects: leather, paper, wood, felt, brushed metal, glass. It peaked between the first iPhone (2007) and iOS 6 (2012), led by Apple under Steve Jobs and Scott Forstall and copied everywhere on Dribbble. Its bet was that touchscreens and new software felt more familiar when they looked like things you already knew how to use. A notes app looked like a legal pad, a calendar had a stitched leather binding, and a switch looked like it would click. iOS 7 (2013) swapped it for flat design, but the craft behind it (light, depth, material, tactility) is still the basis of good visual UI.

## Key characteristics

- **Real materials rendered in pixels**: wood grain, stitched leather, linen, green baize felt, brushed aluminium, torn paper, each with texture noise and a light source.
- **One consistent light source from above**: highlights on top edges (`inset 0 1px 0` white), shadows below, and gradients that get darker toward the bottom.
- **Letterpress and engraved type**: text pressed into a surface with a 1px `text-shadow`, white below on light materials and black above on dark ones.
- **Glossy, bevelled controls**: buttons with a hard highlight line at 50%, inner shadows, rounded pills, and physical toggle switches with ON/OFF text.
- **Objects on a desk**: polaroids held on with tape and paper clips, sticky notes with curled corners, index cards, Dymo label tape, pencils, ribbons.
- **Mixed typography**: Helvetica-style UI type for the chrome, plus handwritten faces (Marker Felt in Notes) wherever a "person" is writing.
- **Motion that feels mechanical**: little animation overall, but controls slide, press down and snap like real hardware.

## How this redesign applies it

- **The page is a walnut desk** made from a stretched SVG `feTurbulence` wood grain, plank seams and a warm overhead light. A brushed-metal Leopard toolbar sits on top, with traffic-light buttons and an Aqua segmented control as the in-page nav.
- **The hero is a stitched leather notebook** (dashed "thread" border, debossed `AH` monogram, a screwed-on brass nameplate holding the `h1`). Inside is an iOS Notes-style legal pad with a leather header, torn paper stubs, blue rules, a red double margin and Kalam standing in for Marker Felt. A red-marker scribble and circle mark up the headline. A pencil and a ribbon bookmark lie on the desk.
- **A working iOS 5 toggle** (`role="switch"`) switches the pad from handwriting to typeset Inter, so the physical switch has a real job.
- **About is a letter on the desk**: letterpress heading, a "Personal" rubber stamp, page-curl shadows and a Caveat signature. Next to it is a polaroid pinned with an SVG paper clip.
- **Projects are laid out on a Game Center-style felt table** with a wooden rail and a stitched seam. Each project has a taped polaroid screenshot, a ruled index card, and a stack written on black, red, blue and green Dymo embossed tape. "Visit website" is a glossy blue iOS button.
- **Contact is a Find My Friends-style leather panel** with an engraved metal plate, a pinned sticky note and calculator keys for the tech list. The links sit in an iPhone 4 whose screen shows a pinstriped iOS grouped table view. The footer is a brushed-metal strip with an arrow-shaped iOS back button.
