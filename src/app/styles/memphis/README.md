# Memphis Design

Memphis was a Milan design collective that Ettore Sottsass founded in December 1980. It was named after the Bob Dylan song that was playing the night they met. Its first show, in September 1981, pushed back against the "good taste" of modernism: furniture in plastic laminate printed with cheap, suburban-looking patterns, colours that clash on purpose, and shapes that look more like toys than tools. Nathalie Du Pasquier, George Sowden, Peter Shire, Michele De Lucchi and others worked on the idea that decoration, humour and emotion matter as much as function.

## Key characteristics

- **Clashing colour:** hot pink, turquoise, lemon yellow and electric blue share a surface with pastels (lilac, mint, peach), and black ties them together.
- **Pattern as surface:** dots, grids, stripes, zigzags, confetti/terrazzo and Sottsass's "Bacterio" squiggle laminate are applied flatly, like printed plastic, not as illusions.
- **Primitive geometry:** circles, triangles, half-discs, cylinders and lightning bolts are stacked into totems (Peter Shire's furniture, Sottsass's *Carlton* bookcase).
- **Bold black outlines** and flat hard shadows, with no gradients and no blur. Everything reads like a cut-out or a cartoon.
- **Asymmetric, irreverent layout:** things are rotated a few degrees, overlap and sit off-grid. The composition is balanced but never neat.
- **Chunky, geometric display type** in poster-like weights, often next to a bouncy script. The mix of type styles is itself a bit of anti-taste.
- **Playfulness:** objects wobble and wiggle, and nothing takes itself too seriously.

## How this redesign applies it

- **Hero sculpture:** the hero's right half is a Shire-style totem built from CSS and SVG shapes: a dotted sun, a grid slab, a pink triangle, a lilac arc on a striped column, a Bacterio block, a lightning bolt, a squiggle and a blue ring, all standing on a two-tone plinth.
- **Patterns:** every pattern is pure CSS gradients or inline SVG data URIs, including the terrazzo confetti background, the Bacterio worms (black on white, and a multicolour-on-black version in the contact section), zigzags, dots, grids and stripes. Each project screenshot sits on a slab with a different pattern.
- **Type:** *Rubik Mono One* is the blocky display face. The surname uses a turquoise fill with a black stroke and a hard offset shadow. *Shrikhand* is the cheeky script for "Hi, I'm Atrin!" and "me, me, me!", and *Poppins* keeps body copy geometric but readable.
- **Rotated, outlined cards:** cards are tilted, bordered in 3px black and dropped on hard 8px shadows. They overlap each other and the images, as in a Du Pasquier collage. Body copy always sits on a solid white or yellow panel so it stays legible over the patterns.
- **Stickers, pills and chips:** the hero's key words (VIBRANT, FAST, SCALABLE) are coloured stickers. Nav items, links and tech chips are fat outlined pills in rotating clashing colours that tilt and pop on hover.
- **Motion:** a spinning spark, a wobbling ring and a wiggling squiggle, all CSS keyframes that stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
